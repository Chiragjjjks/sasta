Sasta v0.3.0 adds lists for the things you buy again, live Blinkit prices, and a cart that finds the cheapest total after delivery fees.

### Lists

- Tap the bookmark on any product to save it to a list. Make as many lists as you like, such as Office snacks or Monthly groceries, each with its own emoji.
- Open a list, tick the products you need this time, and tap **Add 5 to cart**. Sasta checks fresh prices for just those, adds them with your usual quantities, and opens the cart.
- **Same as last time** ticks what you picked on your previous visit. Badges show price drops, what you bought last time, and products no app has right now.
- Sort by often bought, cheapest or A–Z. Move or copy products between lists, rename lists, share a list as text, and back up or restore all lists as a file.

### Live Blinkit prices

- In the Android app, Blinkit results are live for your location. Other apps say **Not live yet** instead of showing sample prices next to real ones.
- Real Blinkit pack sizes and multipacks are recognised, including flavours like West Indies Hot n Sweet Chilli.

### Cheapest cart after fees

- Sasta reads Blinkit's real delivery and handling charges from a live bill, for example ₹25 delivery below ₹99 and ₹2 handling.
- The cart shows each app's items, delivery and handling, and offers **Cheapest after fees** when one delivery beats splitting your cart. It tells you how much switching saves.

### Check out on Blinkit with one tap

- **Open in Blinkit with these items** fills your Blinkit cart and location, then opens Blinkit checkout inside Sasta. Your account, coupons and offers apply there.
- UPI payment links open your UPI app.

### Connected apps (beta)

- The person icon lets you sign in to Blinkit, Zepto, BigBasket, Flipkart and Amazon on their own pages, and to Swiggy Instamart through Swiggy's official connection. You type your number and OTP on their page, never in Sasta.
- This is new and hasn't been tested on many phones yet. If a sign-in doesn't stick, Disconnect and try again.

### Install

1. Download `Sasta-v0.3.0-android.apk` below on your Android phone.
2. Open it. It updates v0.2.0 in place and keeps your cart.
3. Optional: check the file against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
