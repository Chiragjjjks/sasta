# Sasta v0.7.0

A clearer app in light and dark: search a product, see every app's price next to its name, add it, and the cart keeps itself current. Lists now show where to buy each item and the cheapest way to buy the whole list.

## Search, compare, cart

- **Light or dark.** Account → Appearance: System, Light or Dark. Text is easier to read in both, and the tab labels are no longer cut off.
- **Every price says which app it is from.** Each search result lists its three cheapest apps with their coloured mark, delivery time and how much more each costs (+₹2), then **Add · BigBasket**. Once in the cart, the button becomes a quantity stepper.
- **Less above the results.** Recent searches hide after a search, the progress dots are app marks, and the best-value card only shows when it is not already the first result.
- **Compare lets you choose the app.** Tap an app to buy it there; **Add** shows the price and app. After adding, the bar shows the quantity and **View cart · items · total**. The arrow on each row opens that app.
- **The cart keeps itself current.** Opening it, coming back to Sasta, or changing your location re-checks prices when the last check is over 5 minutes old. Items show "↓ ₹3 since last check", and a banner counts changed prices.
- **The cart shows the cheapest way first.** A card at the top shows the total, how it splits across apps and the MRP saving, with one tap to switch between the cheapest mix, fewer deliveries (when fees are known) and one app only. Below it, one card per app lists its items. Each item shows the other apps' prices ("Also Zepto same · Blinkit +₹2"), and tapping that switches the app. If an app you picked runs out, the item moves to the next cheapest and says so.
- **Home explains the flow:** Search → Compare apps → Add to cart, and you order and pay in each app.

## Lists

- **Every item on every app.** Under each product in a list, the apps are ranked cheapest first: price each, delivery time, and how much more each app costs (+₹5). The cheapest is marked. Apps where it is out of stock are named.
- **Choose an app per item.** Tap any app under an item to buy it there instead. It is marked "Your pick" and the totals update. Tap it again, or tap the cheapest, to go back.
- **The plan comes first.** A card at the top shows the total for the whole list, a coloured bar of how it splits across apps, and how much it saves over buying everything on one app. Pills switch between **Cheapest mix** and **Only Zepto / Only Blinkit / …**, with how many items each app has.
- **By app.** A second view lists what to order where: "From Instamart: Amul Taaza × 2, Maggi × 1 · ₹118", each line opening that product in the app. Items no app has are listed separately with a Search button.
- **Prices check themselves.** Opening a list checks every item on every app. Searches from the last 10 minutes are reused; pull down or tap Refresh to check again.
- **Pick some.** Tick items to plan and add only those. "Same as last time" picks what you bought last.
- **Tell apps apart at a glance.** Each app has its own coloured mark: Blinkit yellow B, Zepto purple Z, Instamart orange I, BigBasket green bb, Flipkart blue F, Amazon dark a. Used in lists, search, product comparison and Account.
- List cards show each list's cheapest total and which apps it comes from. **Save to list** shows what is already in each list.

Your app choices are saved with the list, go into the cart with the items, and are kept in list backups.

## Current limits

Totals are before delivery and handling fees, except where the cart already knows Blinkit's fees. Flipkart Minutes and Amazon Now prices still depend on your account and delivery address. Always confirm the final total in the shopping app.

Install over the previous version to keep your lists, cart and accounts. Do not uninstall first.
