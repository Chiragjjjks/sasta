Sasta v0.3.2 fixes the sign-in problems you reported and makes the app lighter and cleaner on small phones.

### Sign-in comes back to Sasta

- After you enter the OTP, the sign-in page closes by itself and returns to Sasta. It now also reads the site's own "signed in" state, not just its cookies. This fixes Zepto staying on its page after login.
- Back in Sasta, every new connection is tested once. You see **Zepto connected · test passed**, or what went wrong.
- Connected apps show the last test result and a **Test** button.

### Instamart

- The step where Swiggy hands the sign-in back to Sasta is caught three different ways now, so it can't get stuck on a blank or error page after the OTP.
- If Swiggy's official sign-in still fails, you see why, and **Or sign in on Swiggy's website** connects through Swiggy's normal site instead.

### Faster

- Sasta no longer runs Blinkit's or Zepto's whole website hidden in the background. It loads a tiny file from their site instead, which is all live search needs.
- Lighter header and shadows, fewer animations, and result cards only redraw when they change.

### Cleaner results

- Results are always **cheapest first**. The Fastest option is gone.
- Product cards show the full name next to the price, with other apps' prices and the Save and Add buttons on one row below. Checked at 360 px wide.

### Install

1. Download `Sasta-v0.3.2-android.apk` below on your Android phone.
2. Open it. It updates in place and keeps your cart, lists and connected apps.
3. If Zepto or Instamart didn't connect before, open Connected apps and tap **Reconnect** or **Connect**.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
