# Sasta v0.10.0

Search results are now a grid that shows every app's price for every product at once.

- **Two products side by side.** Grocery results, and each product's section in a several-product search, are a two-column grid, so you see twice as many products without scrolling.
- **Every app on the card.** Each product lists every app that sells that pack, cheapest first, one row each: the app, its delivery time, the MRP struck through, the price, and the **price per 100 g** (or per 100 ml, or per piece). The old cards showed the top three apps; now none are hidden.
- **Cheapest at a glance.** The cheapest app's row is tinted green. When two apps charge the same, both are tinted, so a tie reads as a tie. Apps that are out of stock stay on the card, dimmed.
- **Savings and actions on the photo.** The photo shows the saving against MRP (for example **27% OFF**), a Save button for your lists, and **Add**, which turns into a − 1 + counter once the product is in your cart.
- The line above the results now reads **Checked 6 apps · 24 products**.

Tap a product for the full comparison, as before. Lists, the cart and the comparison screen are unchanged.

## Current limits

The grid was checked in the browser preview with sample prices (390 and 360 px wide, light and dark), not yet on a phone with live prices. Everything from [v0.9.1](v0.9.1.md) still applies.

Install over the previous version to keep your lists, cart and accounts. Do not uninstall first.
