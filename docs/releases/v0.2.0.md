Sasta v0.2.0 adds a cart that finds the cheapest way to buy everything on your list. Add products from different searches, and Sasta picks the cheapest app for each one.

> **Sample data.** Prices, stock and delivery times are still generated, not live. Live search arrives in a later release.

### Cheapest cart

- Tap **Add** on any result, or **Add to cart** in the compare sheet. Add Lay's, then search Maggi and add that too.
- Each item comes from its cheapest in-stock app by default. That selection is the **Cheapest cart**.
- See the total, what you save against MRP, and how many apps the order is split across.

### Or keep it to one app

- The cart lists options like **All on Zepto · ₹60 · +₹4**, so you can see what a single delivery costs compared with the cheapest cart.
- Apps that are missing an item say so, for example "Has 2 of 3".
- You can also move a single item to a different app.
- If your pick costs more, **Switch to cheapest cart · save ₹X** puts it back in one tap.

### Also

- Change quantities, refresh prices, or clear the cart.
- The cart bar at the bottom shows your item count, total, and whether you're on the cheapest cart.
- The cart is saved on your phone between sessions.

Delivery fees, handling charges and minimum order values aren't included yet. They need you to be signed in to each app, which is the next thing being built.

### Install

1. Download `Sasta-v0.2.0-android.apk` below on your Android phone.
2. Open it. If you installed v0.1.0, it updates in place.
3. Optional: check the file against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
