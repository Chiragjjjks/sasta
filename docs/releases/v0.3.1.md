Sasta v0.3.1 gets Connected apps ready for you to sign in to all your shopping apps.

### Connect all your apps in one go

- Tap the person icon, then **Connect all apps**. Sasta opens each app's own sign-in page in turn: Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, BigBasket, Flipkart and Amazon.
- On each page, tap Login and enter your number and OTP there. Sasta never sees them.
- For Blinkit, Zepto, Amazon and Swiggy's website, the page closes by itself once you're signed in. For the others, tap **I'm signed in**.
- Cancel any sign-in to stop the walk-through. You can connect the rest later.

### Sign-ins that are checked

- Blinkit shows **Connected as •••• 1234**, so you can see which number is linked. Its delivery location is set before the login form, which Blinkit asks for first.
- Zepto sign-ins are now checked on zepto.com, where Zepto moved. Before this fix a Zepto sign-in could never be confirmed.
- Amazon sign-ins are confirmed by Amazon's own sign-in cookies.
- For Flipkart and BigBasket, Sasta learns which cookies mean "signed in" on your phone, then spots when the session expires.
- Expired sessions show **Reconnect** when you open the app.

### Swiggy Instamart

- Uses Swiggy's official connection first.
- If that doesn't work, **Or sign in on Swiggy's website** connects through Swiggy's normal website instead.

### Install

1. Download `Sasta-v0.3.1-android.apk` below on your Android phone.
2. Open it. It updates v0.3.0 in place and keeps your cart and lists.
3. Optional: check the file against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
