Sasta v0.1.0 is the first installable preview. It is the full search and compare experience on your phone, running on sample prices while live platform search is built.

> **Sample data.** Prices, stock and delivery times in this build are generated, not live. The footer of the app says so. Live search arrives in a later release.

### Search once, compare everywhere

- Type a product and Sasta checks Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, BigBasket, Flipkart Minutes and Amazon Now in parallel.
- Each app reports its own progress, and results appear as each app answers.
- Recent searches sit under the search bar for one-tap repeats.

### Results that make sense

- The same pack, spelled differently on each app, is grouped into one card.
- **Best value** shows the lowest price per 100 g across every flavor and size.
- Filter by flavor, switch pack size, and sort by cheapest or fastest.
- Searching `lays cream onion` opens straight on Cream & Onion.

### Compare sheet

- Tap any card to see every app's price, MRP, delivery time and stock side by side.
- See how much you save against the priciest app.
- **Open** jumps to that app's search for the product.

### Location

- Set your delivery area and pincode, or use your current location.
- Your location and recent searches are remembered on the phone. There is no account and no server.

### Install

1. Download `Sasta-v0.1.0-android.apk` below on your Android phone.
2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
3. Optional: check the file against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
