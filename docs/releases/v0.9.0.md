# Sasta v0.9.0

Food delivery: type a dish or a restaurant and see what it costs on Zomato and Swiggy, side by side.

- **A new Food tab.** The Search tab is now **Grocery**; **Food** sits next to it. Type `biryani` or `meghana` and Sasta searches Zomato and Swiggy at your delivery location at the same time.
- **The same dish, matched across apps.** Sasta matches each dish at the same restaurant on both apps, even when they spell the name, branch or portion differently ("Hotel Aditya- Mahalakshmipuram", "Serves 1-2"). Each dish card shows every app's price, delivery time and offer, cheapest first, with a veg or non-veg mark.
- **Honest ties.** Most restaurants charge the same menu price on both apps. When they do, Sasta says **Same price** and points to the faster app instead of calling one of them cheaper.
- **Price a whole order.** Tap a dish to open the restaurant: its menu lists each app's price side by side. Add dishes and Sasta totals the order on every app, then **Open in Zomato** or **Open in Swiggy** takes you to that restaurant to order and pay there.
- **Ownly** (by Rapido) and **Swish** have no website to read, so they show as **App only** with a button that opens their app.
- **Easier to install.** The notification-reading service from v0.8.0 is gone, because it could make Google Play Protect block the install. Connect all apps still types your number into every app once; you now type each OTP on the app's own page. The app also no longer asks for storage, overlay, biometric or vibration access.

## Current limits

Zomato and Swiggy food search was checked in desktop Chrome at Rajajinagar, not yet on a phone. Swiggy doesn't show its delivery fee in search, so order totals there end in "+". Swiggy's full menu is blocked for logged-out visitors, so a Swiggy restaurant shows only the dishes found by your search. Sasta never places food orders or pays.

Install over the previous version to keep your lists, cart and accounts. Do not uninstall first.
