# Sasta v0.9.0

Compare several groceries at once, and a new Food tab that shows what a dish costs on Zomato and Swiggy, side by side.

- **Several products in one search.** In Grocery, type or paste `lays, maggi, amul butter`. Each product becomes a chip and **Compare 3** checks them all. A **Best price for each** card lists every product's cheapest app at the top, and each product gets its own section below with two full price cards and **More matches**. Tap a row in the card to jump to that product. Sasta checks one product at a time, with a short pause between them, so the apps see normal use. You can stop the search, remove a product that hasn't started, or retry one that failed. Up to 5 products; a sixth is refused with a message, never dropped silently. One product works exactly as before.
- **Prices never mix locations.** If you change your delivery area, account or Instamart address after a search, the results dim and say **Search again** instead of showing prices from the old place.

- **A new Food tab.** The Search tab is now **Grocery**; **Food** sits next to it. Type `biryani` or `meghana` and Sasta searches Zomato and Swiggy at your delivery location at the same time.
- **The same dish, matched across apps.** Sasta matches each dish at the same restaurant on both apps, even when they spell the name, branch or portion differently ("Hotel Aditya- Mahalakshmipuram", "Serves 1-2"). Each dish card shows every app's price, delivery time and offer, cheapest first, with a veg or non-veg mark.
- **Honest ties.** Most restaurants charge the same menu price on both apps. When they do, Sasta says **Same price** and points to the faster app instead of calling one of them cheaper.
- **Price a whole order.** Tap a dish to open the restaurant: its menu lists each app's price side by side. Add dishes and Sasta totals the order on every app, then **Open in Zomato** or **Open in Swiggy** takes you to that restaurant to order and pay there.
- **Ownly** (by Rapido) and **Swish** have no website to read, so they show as **App only** with a button that opens their app.
- **Easier to install.** The notification-reading service from v0.8.0 is gone, because it could make Google Play Protect block the install. Connect all apps still types your number into every app once; you now type each OTP on the app's own page. The app also no longer asks for storage, overlay, biometric or vibration access.

## Current limits

The several-product search was checked in the browser preview with sample prices, not yet on a phone with live prices. Pasting a list with line breaks is untested on Android; commas always work. Zomato and Swiggy food search was checked in desktop Chrome at Rajajinagar, not yet on a phone. Swiggy doesn't show its delivery fee in search, so order totals there end in "+". Swiggy's full menu is blocked for logged-out visitors, so a Swiggy restaurant shows only the dishes found by your search. Sasta never places food orders or pays.

Install over the previous version to keep your lists, cart and accounts. Do not uninstall first.
