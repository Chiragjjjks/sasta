# Sasta v0.6.0

Instamart and Zepto now return live prices through their official servers. BigBasket reads its own price list instead of the page layout.

- **Instamart works.** Two bugs meant it never showed a price: Swiggy refuses map coordinates from a signed-in account and needs one of your saved addresses, and its replies did not match the format Sasta expected. Sasta now searches your chosen Swiggy address (Swiggy's own default until you pick one), shows which address in Account, and lets you change it under **Delivery address**. Delivery times come from Swiggy.
- **Zepto connects officially.** **Connect** on Zepto now signs in on Zepto's own page and asks only for read-only access (search and prices, no cart or orders). It searches at the exact location you set in Sasta and shows Zepto's delivery time. A Zepto website sign-in still works as a fallback.
- **BigBasket** reads products, every pack size, prices, MRP and stock from BigBasket's own product list. It checks that you picked a delivery area in BigBasket and that it is the 10-minute bb now service. The page reader stays as a backup.
- **Blinkit** reports a missing location or a blocked request clearly. An account check can no longer interrupt a search that is already running.
- Signed-in Swiggy and Zepto sessions renew themselves without logging you out, and an expired server session is restarted automatically.
- Takes Expo's 58.0.20 patch releases.

## How this was checked

Swiggy and Zepto were signed in for real on 2026-10-28. Sasta's own search code then got 12 matching Instamart products and 10 Zepto products for "amul milk" from the live servers. Blinkit's search and BigBasket's product list were checked in desktop Chrome at Rajajinagar, 560010. Regression tests use trimmed copies of those replies. Publishing still requires the signed APK to pass the Android emulator checks: upgrade, saved data, navigation, sign-in cancel and offline recovery.

## Current limits

- Flipkart Minutes still needs an exact Flipkart delivery address. A full Minutes price list has not been seen on the website yet.
- Amazon Now shows prices only if your Amazon web session offers a Now store. Fresh and regular Amazon listings are excluded.
- Instamart searches a saved Swiggy address, not Sasta's map point. Pick the address that matches your Sasta location.
- Always confirm stock, fees and the final total in the shopping app before you pay.

Install over the previous version to keep your lists, cart and accounts. Do not uninstall first.
