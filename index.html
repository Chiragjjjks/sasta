    04Under the hood

    Careful about prices. Careful about you.

    Six apps, in parallel

    Each app is queried at once with timeouts, retries and a short cache. One slow app never holds up the other five.

    Official MCP where it exists

    Instamart and Zepto are read through their official MCP servers, with read-only scopes. The rest run on their own website, inside a hidden page on your phone.

    Brand, variant, pack

    Titles are cleaned and sizes parsed, with brand and flavour synonyms, so "48g" and "48 gm" land on one card.

    No backend

    Your number, sessions and lists live only on the phone, in encrypted storage. There is no Sasta server to leak from or to track you.

    OTP auto-fill, on your terms

    With notification access you grant yourself, Sasta reads the sign-in code and types it into the app the message names. It listens only while you are signing in.

    Never pays, never orders

    Sasta compares, then hands off to the real app. No checkout, no payment, no creating or deleting addresses. Ever.

    05How a search runs

    From one word to one answer.

    1. 01

      Location set

      Your exact pin picks the dark store each app uses. Nothing is searched without it.

    2. 02

      Six apps queried in parallel

      Official MCP or a hidden page per app, each with its own timeout and retry.

    3. 03

      Titles normalised

      Brand, flavour and size synonyms are cleaned so the same pack matches everywhere.

    4. 04

      Grouped

      Brand, then variant, then pack size. Like is compared with like.

    5. 05

      Ranked cheapest in stock

      Out of stock never wins. Ties are marked "same", gaps "+₹2".

    6. 06

      Hand-off to pay

      Open the real app and pay there. Sasta never places the order.

    07FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Does Sasta place orders or pay for me?

    No, never. Sasta compares prices and then opens the real app, where you check out and pay yourself. It does not automate checkout, payment, or creating and deleting addresses.

    Do all six apps work today?

    Blinkit, Instamart, Zepto and BigBasket are verified live. Flipkart Minutes and Amazon Now are in beta: Flipkart needs an exact address on its side, and Amazon only works if your session is offered a Now store. They show their own state in the app instead of guessing.

    Where do my number, sessions and lists go?

    Nowhere. There is no backend. Your number is encrypted on the phone and sessions are kept only on the phone. Instamart and Zepto sign in through their official OAuth flow with read-only scopes; the others use their own website session inside a hidden page.

    Why does it ask for notification access?

    Only to auto-fill sign-in codes. You grant it yourself, it is armed only while you are connecting apps, and the code is passed straight to the app the message names. Skip it and type the OTPs yourself.

    Will it compare food delivery too?

    That is next. Zomato, Swiggy, Ownly by Rapido and Swish are coming soon, so you can find the cheapest biryani the same way you find the cheapest chips. They are not in the app yet.

    Why is it not on the Play Store?

    Sasta is a personal project for one person's use at human-like request rates, shared as a sideloaded APK with the source on GitHub. It is not affiliated with any of the platforms.

    What does it cost? Is there iOS?

    It is free and Android only, for now. You can read the whole thing on GitHub.
